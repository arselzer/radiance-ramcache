# ds41 results and experiment status, November 5, 2026

The overview table reports validated results from `ds41-stable` (compiled runtime `f84b23db`,
`libr4d` SHA-256 prefix `92bd7719`). The new measurements use four RX 9070 XT cards, a 192 GiB
expert cache, 2,048-token chunks and a fixed 131,072-token reservation. They do not qualify every
later `main` runtime merge. This publication changes documentation only.

## Validated prefill admission

Admitting every selected expert into the existing bounded RAM cache avoids repeated drive reads
on later chunks. It changes cache admission, while retaining the same weights, routing and arithmetic.

| Original prompt | Default admission, TTFT | Admit-all, TTFT | Reduction | Measurement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 6,817 tokens | 17.163 s | 13.244 s | 22.8% | one quiet pair, `ab-20261104T115653Z` |
| 47,917 tokens | 100.254 s | 61.789 s | 38.4% | OFF/ON/ON/OFF means, `ab-20261104T121517Z` |

The overview's prefill throughput uses the original replies' `timings.prompt_per_second`, rather
than deriving it from TTFT: 514.86 tok/s for the 6,817-token prompt and 775.67 tok/s mean for the
47,917-token prompt (772.43 and 778.91 in the two runs). The field measures computed prompt tokens
from the first scheduled step to first token, excluding parsing, tokenization and admission wait.
All these requests have zero prefix-cache hits.

After each 47,917-token prefill, the original eight held-out requests ran at concurrency eight.
Admit-all measured 46.50 and 47.83 tok/s total, versus controls of 46.51 and 46.83. Pair means
were 47.16 versus 46.67 tok/s; wave time was 14.00 versus 14.08 seconds. This is a no-regression
guard, without a claim of a repeatable decode gain. Total decode rate is concurrency times the
mean `timings.predicted_per_second`, consistently with the existing table.

Across both windows, all 80 original replies and prompt/generated counts matched their qualified
references, as did all five captured full prefill digests. The recommendation remains specific to
the tested 192 GiB cache budget. Smaller or changing budgets retain the general `LCP_ADMIT=128`
fallback until separately measured. Admit-all at 100K, with prefix-cached agent turns, or together
with the optional expert worker remains unmeasured.

## Checkpoint audit

All 1,119 other neural tensors passed the declared conversion mappings: unchanged BF16/F32 values,
decoded FP8 to BF16, BF16 to F32, and the two derived conversions. The audit covered 16,834,990,528
destination bytes with zero refused entries or mismatches. Independently regenerated integer
Engram controls matched all seven arrays (129,384 words).

Native MXFP4 expert codes and E8M0 scales are preserved. The two ISTA IQ4_NL Engram tables remain
additional quantization. Optimization exactness is against this hybrid imported baseline; neither
the audit nor reply matching establishes equivalence to an independent official runner.
